\begin{abstract}
We specify and test a minimal deterministic two-step agent environment
motivated by predictive forward-only fine-tuning. The environment
uses a synthetic hidden update direction, permits one probe and one
fake update or abstention, and returns a scalar reward. This establishes
an executable environment contract, not model gradient prediction,
learned optimization or a Tunix integration.
\end{abstract}
\section{Prediction and model}
We preregister a reproducibility hypothesis: a bounded synthetic
probe-to-update episode can be exercised safely without loading real
weights or contacting providers. A future experiment may test whether
architecture sketches and representation statistics predict useful
low-rank update directions compared with exact autograd and
orthogonal/gaussian forward-only estimators \cite{parity}.
Our T0 null includes malformed action acceptance or unsanctioned
execution. The synthetic gradient $g$ is task-id-derived.
An action chooses coordinate $i$ for observation $g_i$; the next
action chooses a sign $s\in\{-1,+1\}$ and coordinate $j$ with
reward $r=s g_j-0.1$, or abstains with $r=0$.
\section{Observed results}
On X1 the focused environment tests passed 5/5.
The fixed two-task rewards were 0.75826772 and 0.27795276,
respectively. Neither the JAX nor Tunix module was installed.
No trainable policy or target model was loaded. These synthetic
rewards are not predictive-quality observations.
\section{Limitations and next tests}
Tunix \cite{tunix} is a JAX-based post-training library for supervised
and reinforcement learning. Its policy optimization routines need
not be forward-only even if the target model's update estimator is.
This study does not exercise Tunix APIs, JAX hardware backends,
any inference, architecture transfer, embeddings, private data,
checkpoint quality or external environment actions.
The next phase requires a pinned upstream revision, isolated JAX
CPU installation, sealed train/test lineage and budget-matched
controls. All learned policies remain advisory and cannot grant
provider capacity or production authority.
